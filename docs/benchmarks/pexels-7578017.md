# Pexels clip reconstruction trial

Source: [People going inside a house (Pexels video 7578017)](https://www.pexels.com/video/people-going-inside-a-house-7578017/). The clip is 11.43 seconds, 3840×2160 at 30 fps. Twenty frames were sampled evenly at 1.75 fps and resized to 1280×720 JPEGs. This is an offline reconstruction test; the mobile app currently captures JPEGs and does not ingest MP4 video directly.

## Outcome

The official VGGT worker completed all three runs on the RTX 4070 Ti at a 336-pixel model input. The 20-frame scene exported and loaded in the desktop Viser viewer. Inference was fast and fit comfortably in available VRAM, but the scene was not a usable room reconstruction: the clip mostly shows people entering through one doorway while the camera stays in place. The viewer shows a distorted partial doorway/interior with moving people smeared into the geometry, rather than a navigable reconstruction of the room.

| Frames | Inference time | Peak VRAM | Peak RAM | Exported points |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 8 | 10.52 s | 5,105 MB | 9,363 MB | 263,424 |
| 12 | 8.59 s | 5,148 MB | 10,737 MB | 395,136 |
| 20 | 8.60 s | 5,248 MB | 10,847 MB | 500,000 (export cap) |

VGGT estimated a total camera-center span of about 0.034 scene units and a cumulative camera path of 0.109 units across all 20 frames. These coordinates are unscaled, but they confirm that the input provides almost no useful viewpoint baseline. Extra frames from the same fixed camera do not recover missing views; people moving through the doorway add changing content instead.

## Interpretation

- The GPU worker, point-cloud export, saved-scene manifest, and desktop viewer function end to end on this sample.
- This clip is not a fair test of a phone walking around a room. Its camera barely moves, it covers only an entryway, and people occlude the static scene.
- The app's intended two-second capture cadence would produce only about six frames from this clip, fewer than the eight-frame benchmark minimum. The offline test sampled more densely to exercise the 8/12/20 benchmark.
- A real acceptance test still needs 12–20 overlapping views from a slow walk around one room, with the camera moving and people kept out of the scene where possible.
